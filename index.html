Questions

Don't see your answer? Email titaservices.orders@gmail.com.

Is the food spicy?

Some dishes have a kick. Just let us know when you order and we can make it mild, medium or hot where possible.

What about allergies?

Our home kitchen uses peanuts, wheat, eggs, dairy, fish and shellfish. We can't guarantee anything is allergen-free, so please ask before ordering if you have an allergy.

How do I store and reheat leftovers?

Refrigerate within 2 hours and eat within 3 days. Reheat until steaming hot. Rice and stews also freeze well.

Do you deliver?

Email us and ask. Delivery depends on your location and the size of your order.
